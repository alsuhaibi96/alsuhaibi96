## Abdulrahman Alsuhaibi

**Tech Lead & Product Manager · Senior full-stack engineer** · based in Saudi Arabia

I build and run digital products end to end: from the product decision and the architecture to the
code, the infrastructure bill and the team that ships it. Most of my work is in Laravel and PHP, with
Vue/Nuxt and React on the front end, Filament for admin panels, Asterisk for VoIP and call centers,
and payments/fintech integrations for the Saudi market.

### What I build

- **On-demand and marketplace platforms**: dispatch, real-time driver/provider matching, ETAs, pricing
- **Payments for Saudi Arabia**: mada, cards, Apple Pay and BNPL (Tabby, Tamara, MISpay) behind one interface, toggled without app releases
- **Call centers and telephony**: Asterisk/PJSIP, Arabic IVRs, queues, recording, automated confirmation calls
- **Admin panels and internal tools** with Filament, and the background workers that keep it all moving
- **Cost-aware infrastructure**: replacing per-request SaaS bills with self-hosted, boring, reliable services
